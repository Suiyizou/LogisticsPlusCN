# LogisticsPlusCN editorial images

The four illustrations were generated with built-in ImageGen from briefs prepared by GPT-6 Astra Ultra. They are synthetic editorial scenes. They do not identify real employees, vessels or terminals.

| File               | Scene and composition brief                                                                                              |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| night-port.webp    | Blue-hour terminal, vessel and gantry cranes to the right, amber lights reflected in dark water with room for a caption. |
| loading-bay.webp   | Shaded warehouse, orderly kraft cartons and pallets, cobalt container and an operator near the loading doorway.          |
| open-voyage.webp   | High aerial view of one vessel in deep teal water with a clear wake and open sea around it.                              |
| dispatch-team.webp | Three colleagues reviewing a shipment document in a port-side workspace with visible cranes through the windows.         |

Each scene was generated separately at 1586 × 992. The original PNGs were re-encoded to WebP at quality 84 without cropping. The brief excluded logos, letters, captions, interface elements and artificial telemetry.
